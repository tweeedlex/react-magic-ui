# Card

A liquid glass panel that groups related content, such as a settings group, a widget or a form. Its content is laid out with flexbox.

<Canvas of={Card.Default} />

## Usage

```tsx
import { Card, Switch } from "react-magic-ui";

<Card gap={14}>
  <h3>Control Center</h3>
  <label>
    Wi-Fi <Switch aria-label="Wi-Fi" defaultChecked />
  </label>
</Card>
```

A row of buttons, pushed apart:

```tsx
<Card direction="row" justify="space-between" align="center">
  <span>3 items selected</span>
  <Button destructive>Delete</Button>
</Card>
```

## No glass on glass

iOS 26 never blurs glass on top of glass. Buttons, switches, fields and tabs inside a card notice they are on glass and draw themselves as **inner layers**: a light fill with a thin edge, but no blur of their own. You don't need to set anything for this.

If something inside a card has to be real glass, such as a popover that floats over other content, pass `layer="surface"` to it. To find out from your own component whether it sits on glass, use `useGlassDepth()`.

## Props

All `<div>` props are passed through, and `ref` points to the `<div>`.

- **size** _`"small" | "medium" | "large"`_: padding 12, 20 or 28px and corner radius 18, 26 or 34px. Default `"medium"`.
- **direction** _`"column" | "row"`_: default `"column"`.
- **align** _`"start" | "center" | "end" | "stretch"`_: `align-items`. Default `"stretch"`.
- **justify** _`"start" | "center" | "end" | "space-between" | "space-around"`_: `justify-content`. Default `"start"`.
- **gap** _`number`_: space between children in px. Default `12`.
- **material** _`"glass" | "frosted"`_: `"frosted"` blurs more softly and doesn't bend the light at the edges. Use it behind long text. Default `"glass"`.
- **tone**, **lens**, **layer**: see [Glass](?path=/docs/glass--docs).

## Tips

- Glass needs something behind it. Over a plain colour it just looks grey; it shows best over a photo, a video or a colourful gradient.
- For text that sits right on the card, rely on the ink colour (`tone`). If a busy photo makes the text hard to read, use `tone="light"` and the card gets darker under light text.
